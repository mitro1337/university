Mostenire in CSS

Transmiterea automata a proprietatilor CSS de la elementele parinte catre cele descendente (copii), precum si suprascrierea acestora prin reguli mai specifice.

Arborele de mostenire

<BODY> [color: green]
<UL> [color: green]
<LI> [color: blue] → <P> [color: blue]
<LI> [color: green] → <P> [color: green]
<LI> [color: green]
<P> [color: red]
<OL> [color: green]
<LI> [color: green] → <P> [color: white]
<LI> [color: green] → <P> [color: green]
<LI> [color: yellow] → <P> [color: yellow]

Demonstratie practica

<P> direct in radacina: color: red

  • <LI> cu regula explicita color: blue

    Acest <P> mosteneste culoarea blue de la parintele LI.

  • <LI> mosteneste green de la radacina

    Acest <P> mosteneste culoarea green.

  • <LI> simplu (mosteneste green).
  1. <LI> in lista ordonata (mosteneste green):

    <P> cu regula explicita color: white pe fundal inchis.

  2. <LI> in lista ordonata:

    <P> copil care mosteneste culoarea green.

  3. <LI> cu regula explicita color: yellow

    Acest <P> copil mosteneste culoarea yellow.

Observatii:

  • Proprietati mostenite implicit: color, font-family, font-size, line-height, text-align.
  • Proprietati care NU se mostenesc: border, margin, padding, background-image, width, height.
  • Cuvantul cheie inherit: Permite fortarea mostenirii oricarei proprietati de la parinte (ex: border: inherit;).
